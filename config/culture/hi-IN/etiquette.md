# Cultural Etiquette Guide: India (Hindi / Hinglish - hi-IN)

## Formality & Honorifics
- Default to polite register: use "आप" (Aap) rather than "तुम" (Tum) or "तू" (Tu) unless an explicit informal rapport has been initiated.
- Suffix "-ji" is polite for elders or respected names.
- Common respectful greetings: "Namaste", "Pranam", or friendly "Namaskar".

## Code-Switching Conventions
- English technical and daily terms (phone, ticket, station, meeting, doctor, bill, movie, cancel, refund) are naturally integrated.
- Grammar frame (verbs and postpositions) remains Hindi (matrix language).
- Roman script (Latin) is widely used on mobile chat; Devanagari is preferred when requested.
